Privacy Policy

Pulse Grid · Last updated 21 November 2026

The short version: no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your progress stays on your device. Only leaderboard entries (a random ID, the display name you see in the game, and your game moves and scores) are sent to our server.

What stays on your device

  • Your games in progress, scores, stats, streaks and settings (sound, vibration, screen shake).
  • A random player ID and your display name, used for the leaderboard.

This data is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app (or clearing the site's data in your browser) deletes it.

What is sent to our server

Only when you finish a Classic or Daily game, open World ranks, or change your name, Pulse Grid sends:

  • A random player ID, made up on your device. It is not linked to your name, email, phone, or any account.
  • Your display name. You start with a random fun name (like "Neon Otter 42") and can change it. It is shown publicly on the leaderboard, so please don't use your real full name.
  • Your game moves and the score they produce, plus the time of submission. The server replays your moves to check the score is genuine.

Our server also sees your IP address, as every internet service does. We use it only briefly, to limit how many submissions one device can send each minute. Pulse Grid's own server keeps it in memory only, never writes it to disk, and never stores it with your scores. Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard, short-lived access logs for security and reliability.

What we never collect

  • No accounts, emails, phone numbers, contacts, location, photos, or device identifiers.
  • No advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs, and nothing sold or shared with third parties.

Sharing

When you tap Share or Dare a friend, your device's own share sheet opens with your result. You choose where it goes. A challenge link contains only a game seed and a score.

Children

Pulse Grid is suitable for all ages. It doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your leaderboard entries

Leaderboard entries contain no personal details. To have yours removed, contact us with the display name you used, and we'll delete it.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or to remove a leaderboard entry: pulsegrid26@gmail.com

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
