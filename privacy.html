Privacy Policy

Pulse Grid · Last updated 22 November 2026

The short version: no account, no sign-up, and the game itself sends nothing to us. Your progress stays on your device. Pulse Grid is free because it shows ads, and the ads are provided by Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below.

What stays on your device

  • Your games in progress, scores, stats, streaks and settings (sound, vibration, screen shake).

This data is stored only on your device and never sent to us. Uninstalling the app (or clearing the site's data in your browser) deletes it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The Android and iPhone apps show ads from Google AdMob: a short video you can choose to watch to keep a game going, and now and then a full-screen ad between games. The web version has no ads.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ads software in the app may collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as your phone's advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iPhone, only if you allow tracking);
  • IP address (which gives an approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads;
  • Diagnostics, such as ad loading errors and performance.

This data goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices:

  • In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and other places where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before ads are personalised. You can change your answer any time from Menu → Privacy choices.
  • On iPhone, you can allow or refuse tracking when asked, or later in Settings → Privacy → Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads).

What we never collect

  • No accounts, emails, phone numbers, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • No analytics or tracking of our own. We don't run a server for the game and never receive your data.

Sharing

When you tap Share or Dare a friend, your device's own share sheet opens with your result. You choose where it goes. A challenge link contains only a game seed and a score.

Children

Pulse Grid is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads in the app are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences with parental guidance (PG).
